About

I started in Electrical Engineering — circuits, systems, logic, and the particular mystery of things failing five minutes before a deadline. Tracing a fault back to its source is still how I debug a layout.

Then I spent a stretch in sales. Cold calls, objections, and the unglamorous business of getting a stranger to care. That's where I learned a page isn't finished when it looks right — it's finished when it convinces someone.

Now I build in Webflow. Same instinct, better tools: work out how a thing functions, then work out how it should look, feel, and move. Outside the browser I bake, which is design with a worse undo button.
